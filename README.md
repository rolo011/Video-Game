# Shooter Retro Game

**Shooter Retro Game** is a retro-inspired FPS that combines classic aesthetics with a mix of 2D and 3D gameplay.

Face off against a dangerous opponent that tracks your movements and shoots to eliminate you. Move strategically, use your weapon, dodge incoming attacks, and fight to survive.

Get ready for an intense shooting challenge where your goal is simple: **defeat your opponent and win the match.**

**Welcome to Shooter Retro Game!**
